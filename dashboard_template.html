Method & provenance

Prompt: positive is predominantly favorable, negative is predominantly unfavorable, and neutral is factual, non-evaluative, or genuinely balanced/mixed with no dominant sentiment. Only title and text are sent. The model never sees stars or the rating-derived class. A 3-star rating is a neutral evaluation label, but its wording may express positive or negative sentiment.

Audit: only the prescribed sentiment and emotion labels from a completed joint final answer are accepted. Truncated, missing, or malformed answers get one higher-budget attempt, then UNKNOWN. Unknowns count against accuracy and recall and appear in filtering. Final answers, completion status, model ID, request IDs, token usage, and the sample manifest are saved locally. The sample is reproducible; repeated model inference is not guaranteed to produce identical answers.

Scope: sentiment is evaluated against rating-derived proxies; primary emotion compares the joint LLM output against NRC word counts without emotion gold labels. All visuals and full-review details are embedded and work without a server or network.
